Privacy Policy for Granby

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Granby is a jiu-jitsu training app. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as importantly, what it does not.

Data we do not collect

Granby has no backend server, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service. We do not track you, and no data about your use of the app is transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself.

Data stored on your device

The following information is created when you use Granby and stays in the app's private storage on your device only, unless you delete the app or use "Start over" in Settings, which erases it:

  • Your belt rank and training reminders
  • Your training history and streak (dates, drills completed)
  • Class logs you choose to record (type, duration, rounds, notes)
  • Injuries you choose to log, used only to adapt that day's session on your device

None of this leaves your device. We cannot see it, and we do not ask you to create an account.

Subscriptions

Premium features are offered as an auto-renewing subscription, processed entirely by Apple's App Store. Granby never sees or stores your payment details — that relationship is between you and Apple, governed by Apple's own privacy practices. Granby only learns whether a subscription is currently active, which stays on your device.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device using iOS's notification system. No notification content is sent from or through any server.

If you contact us

If you use the in-app feedback form, it opens your own mail app with a message addressed to us. We receive whatever you choose to write, plus basic technical details (app version, iOS version, device model) to help investigate bugs. We use this only to respond to you and improve the app, and do not share it with third parties.

Children

Granby is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and, for any material change, we will note it in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open Granby, go to Settings, and use "Send feedback" — it reaches the developer directly.
